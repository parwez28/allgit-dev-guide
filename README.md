## What is allgit.dev?

**AllGit.dev** is an online platform focused on one thing: helping developers
acquire **established GitHub accounts with genuine history**. Instead of
starting from a blank profile, buyers can step into an account that already
carries months or years of contributions, merged pull requests, followers,
and rare profile badges.

The core idea is simple. On GitHub, your profile is your résumé. Recruiters,
clients, collaborators, and even automated systems judge a developer by their
contribution graph, repository history, and badges before they ever read a CV.
Building that kind of credibility organically takes years of consistent work.
AllGit.dev compresses that timeline into a single purchase: you choose an
account tier, pay, receive full credentials, and complete a transfer checklist
that makes the account exclusively yours.

Every account listed on the platform is described as **hand-verified for
authentic activity** — real contribution patterns, real repositories, real
stars, and documented badges — with delivery typically completed in minutes
and full ownership transferred to the buyer.

## Why aged accounts matter

A brand-new GitHub profile starts with nothing: no green squares, no merged
pull requests, no followers, no badges. An aged account changes the equation
on day one:

- **Instant credibility** — a contribution graph stretching back months or
years signals experience at a glance.
- **Trust with people and platforms** — clients, hiring managers, and
maintainers respond differently to an established profile than to a fresh
one.
- **Practical platform advantages** — older, active accounts generally face
fewer friction points: higher API rate limits, fewer spam-filter false
positives, and smoother use of CI/CD tooling.
- **Badges that can't be earned anymore** — some GitHub achievements are
frozen in time. No amount of new activity can earn the Arctic Code Vault or
Mars 2020 badges today; they only exist on accounts that were active during
those historic windows.

In short, an aged account is a shortcut around *waiting*, not around skill.
Your code and expertise remain your own — the account simply gives them the
stage they deserve from the first push.

## GitHub badges, explained

Badges are GitHub's official achievements — small icons on a profile that
tell a story about its history. Some are earnable in weeks; others are
impossible to earn at all anymore:

- **Arctic Code Vault Contributor** — awarded for code archived in the 2020
Arctic snapshot. Can never be earned again.
- **Mars 2020 Contributor** — for code that powered the Mars 2020 helicopter
mission software. Frozen in time.
- **Pull Shark** — earned through merged pull requests, with tiers scaling to
dozens of merged PRs.
- **Pair Extraordinaire** — for co-authored commits via pair programming.
- **Galaxy Brain** — for accepted answers in GitHub Discussions.
- **Starstruck** — awarded as your repositories earn stars (16 / 128 / 512 /
4096 tiers).

Full guide: [`docs/github-badges-guide.md`](docs/github-badges-guide.md)

## How buying works

The purchase flow is designed to go from browsing to full ownership in four
steps, usually within an hour:

1. **Pick your account** — choose the age, badge mix, and history depth that
fits your goals. Custom sourcing (specific years, follower counts, badge
combos) is available on request.
2. **Pay securely** — checkout with cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT
and more) through an encrypted payment flow.
3. **Receive credentials** — login details arrive by email and Telegram
within minutes of payment confirmation.
4. **Secure and own it** — follow the transfer checklist: change the
password, add your email, enable two-factor authentication, save recovery
codes, and review active sessions.

Deep dive: [`docs/how-it-works.md`](docs/how-it-works.md)

## Safety and transfer guarantee

Every sale is positioned as a **full ownership transfer**, not a shared
login. The buyer receives the account email credentials and GitHub password,
plus a step-by-step checklist to replace every recovery method. Accounts are
sourced and maintained by the platform's own team, old sessions are stripped,
credentials rotated, and a **replacement warranty** covers access issues
within the warranty period.
