# How Buying a GitHub Account Works on allgit.dev

The process is designed to take you from browsing to full, exclusive
ownership in four steps. Most customers complete everything in under an hour.

---

## Step 1 — Pick your account

Browse the account tiers (Fresh, Aged, Veteran, Legend) and choose the age,
badge mix, and history depth that fits your goals. Every listing shows exactly
what is on the profile — contribution history, repositories, stars, followers,
and badges — so there are no surprises.

Need something specific? A particular creation year, a minimum follower
count, or an exact badge combination can be sourced on request. Message the
platform with your requirements and they'll check current inventory for you.

**Tip:** Decide who will be looking at the profile before you choose. A
freelancer bidding on contracts needs visible credibility (Aged or Veteran);
a bot account just needs a working login (Fresh).

---

## Step 2 — Pay securely

Checkout uses **cryptocurrency** — Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, and other major
coins — through an encrypted payment flow. Payments confirm automatically
on-chain, which means:

- No waiting for manual approval.
- No sharing of sensitive card details.
- A private purchase that doesn't touch traditional payment rails.

Prices are listed in USD, and the crypto equivalent is calculated at
checkout.

---

## Step 3 — Receive credentials

Within minutes of payment confirmation, your login credentials arrive by
email and Telegram. For premium tiers, the team runs a final manual quality
check before handover — verifying the history, badges, and account standing
one last time — but delivery is still same-day, guaranteed.

What you receive:

- The GitHub account username and password.
- Access to the email address attached to the account.
- Step-by-step transfer guidance.

---

## Step 4 — Secure it and own it

This is the most important step. Every sale is a **full ownership transfer**,
not a shared login, and the transfer checklist makes sure of it:

1. **Change the password** to something only you know.
2. **Add your personal email** as the primary address.
3. **Enable two-factor authentication** (2FA) on the account.
4. **Generate fresh recovery codes** and store them somewhere safe.
5. **Review active sessions** and revoke anything you don't recognize.
6. **Check connected apps and SSH keys** — remove anything left over.

The platform only sells accounts it fully controls, which means there is no
previous owner who could attempt a recovery. Before listing, old sessions are
stripped, credentials are rotated, and the attached email is verified as
fully transferable. Once you've completed the checklist, there is no
technical path for anyone but you to access the account.

---

## The transfer guarantee

Your purchase is backed by a **replacement warranty**: if the account
develops any access issue within the warranty period, it is replaced with an
equivalent or better profile free of charge. If a working account can't be
delivered at all, you get a refund.

Support runs 24/7 via Telegram and WhatsApp, staffed by real people. Most
issues are reported as resolved within hours.

---

## Quick recap

| Step | What happens | Typical time |
|------|--------------|--------------|
| 1. Pick | Choose tier, age, badges | Minutes |
| 2. Pay | Crypto checkout, auto-confirmed | Minutes |
| 3. Receive | Credentials via email + Telegram | Minutes |
| 4. Secure | Transfer checklist, 2FA, recovery codes | ~30 minutes |

Total: usually under an hour from browsing to exclusive ownership.
