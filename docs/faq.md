# Frequently Asked Questions

## How fast is delivery?

Credentials are typically delivered within minutes of payment confirmation,
via email and Telegram. Premium tiers go through a final manual quality
check first, but delivery is still same-day, guaranteed.

## Which payment methods are accepted?

Cryptocurrency: Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, and other major coins, through an
encrypted checkout flow. Payments confirm automatically on-chain — no manual
approval waiting, no card details shared.

## What exactly do I receive?

The GitHub account credentials (username and password), access to the email
address attached to the account, and a step-by-step transfer checklist that
walks you through making the account exclusively yours.

## Is the transfer really full ownership?

Yes — that's the core of the model. You change the password, add your own
email, enable two-factor authentication, generate fresh recovery codes, and
review active sessions. The platform only sells accounts it fully controls,
so there is no previous owner who could attempt a recovery. Old sessions are
stripped and credentials rotated before listing.

## What if something goes wrong after purchase?

Purchases are backed by a **replacement warranty**: if the account develops
any access issue within the warranty period, it's replaced with an equivalent
or better profile free of charge. If a working account can't be delivered at
all, you get a refund. Support is available 24/7 via Telegram and WhatsApp.

## Can I request a specific account?

Yes. If you need a particular creation year, follower count, or badge
combination, message the platform with your requirements. Custom sourcing is
available — if it's in inventory, you can buy it.

## Are the account histories real?

Every account is described as hand-verified for authentic activity: natural
contribution patterns, real repositories and stars, and documented badges.
Listings show exactly what's on the profile before you pay.

## What's the difference between the tiers?

- **Fresh (0–6 months):** clean slate, verified email, most affordable —
ideal for secondary or bot accounts.
- **Aged (1–2 years):** real contribution history and early badges — the
sweet spot for freelancers and job seekers.
- **Veteran (3–4 years):** deep history with rare badges like Arctic Code
Vault — for agencies, founders, and maximum authority.
- **Legend (5+ years):** the rarest tier, with frozen-in-time badges and
often an existing follower base — for collectors and high-stakes use.

See [account-tiers.md](account-tiers.md) for the full breakdown.

## Which badges should I look for?

The rarest are the time-locked ones: **Arctic Code Vault Contributor** (2020
Arctic archive) and **Mars 2020 Contributor** (Mars helicopter mission
software). Neither can ever be earned again. Earned badges like **Pull
Shark**, **Pair Extraordinaire**, **Galaxy Brain**, and **Starstruck** signal
genuine contribution history. See
[github-badges-guide.md](github-badges-guide.md) for the complete guide.

## How do I contact support?

Via Telegram and WhatsApp, 24/7, staffed by real people. Most issues are
resolved within hours.

## Is buying an aged account a substitute for skill?

No — and that's an important distinction. The account gives your existing
work instant credibility; it doesn't write code for you. Freelancers still
need to deliver, job seekers still need to pass interviews, and contributors
still need good PRs. What the account changes is how seriously people take
that work from the first impression.
